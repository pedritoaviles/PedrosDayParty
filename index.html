About the party

IT STARTED
WITH A TERRACE

Some men are born great. Some achieve greatness. And some just have a really good sound system and a terrace that gets afternoon sun until 10 PM. Pedro is the third type.

Pedro's Day Party is the event that happens when you combine a questionable number of bass cabinets, neighbours who've apparently given up, and a group of friends with genuinely excellent taste in music.

The concept is simple: sun, concrete, good drinks, and beats that move from deep house through peak-time techno and back again. When the sun sets, the terrace closes, and the whole thing mutates into something else entirely — an after party with no end time, no open decks, and Pedro playing techno until further notice.

This is not a festival. This is not a club night. This is Pedro's house. The only VIP area is Pedro's kitchen, and you're only getting in there if you brought snacks.

Phase II

THE AFTER
PARTY

When the last person leaves the terrace and the door closes behind them, something shifts. The sun is gone. The house music is gone. The small talk is gone. What remains is the floor, the speakers, and Pedro.

The after party is a different animal entirely. Indoors only. The terrace becomes off-limits — not because of noise, not because of the neighbours, but because this part of the night demands a ceiling. It demands walls. It demands a room that can hold what Pedro is about to do to it.

There will be surprise spots for dancing — corners of the apartment that you didn't think were dance floors until they were. A speaker in a corridor. A cleared bedroom. The kitchen, if it comes to that. The whole flat becomes the venue.

Pedro will play a full techno set. Then he will play another. The last one has no scheduled end time. The last one ends when it ends. Which, historically, has been defined as "when everyone has either left or passed out on a couch in a very satisfied way."

Guest DJ Protocol

USB with Rekordbox library strongly preferred. Format: exFAT. No SSD. Arrive 10 mins before your set. Pedro will show you the mixer, but briefly — he has drinks to attend to. Do not touch the master volume. That's Pedro's domain. Also: read the room. This is a day party, not a warehouse rave. Build your set accordingly. Have fun.

RIDER REQUIREMENTS

Standard rider applies: cold beer within arm's reach of the DJ booth at all times. One sparkling water. A human person Pedro can ask "does this sound okay?" without them saying "yeah sounds great" just to be nice. That's it. Pedro does not request a fruit platter, premium spirits, or a driver. He does request that you do not DJ with a phone jack adapter. We have standards.

THE TERRACE

Rua Leite de Vasconcelos 34 · RC Esq · Lisbon

The venue

You've seen the lineup. You've read the FAQ. Now meet the star of the show: the terrace. A sun-drenched concrete patio tucked behind a residential building in Lisbon, framed by a spectacularly weathered blue wall that's been peeling since at least the early 2000s and looks better for it every year.

String lights strung across the width. Rattan chairs that are more comfortable than they look. Yellow-painted steps that double as seating, a stage, and a DJ monitor stand depending on the hour. Black iron railing. The kind of place that doesn't look like much in a photo and feels exactly right in person.

The terrace gets direct afternoon sun from around 2PM, which is precisely when the party starts. The blue wall reflects heat. The string lights come on at dusk. By 9PM it's the best place in Lisbon. By midnight it's a memory you'll be telling people about.
